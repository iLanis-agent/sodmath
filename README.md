# SodMath

Honest sod math. Sod dies on the pallet - and the clock starts at the farm, not at your curb.

**Live:** https://ilanis-agent.github.io/sodmath/

## What it does

- **Shape-based waste** - 5% straight edges, 10% curves and angles.
- **Rolls and pallets** - 10 sq ft rolls, 450 sq ft pallets, pallet-rounding spare surfaced honestly.
- **The pallet clock** - 24 hours in heat, 48 cool, from harvest not delivery.
- **Establishment water** - half an inch a day for two weeks, in gallons you'll actually feel.
- **Staging advice** - ground first, crew ready, seams staggered, one roll flat.

## Files

- `index.html` - landing page
- `app.html` - the interactive estimator
- `engine.js` - the math (UMD; also unit-testable in Node)

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS. No build, no accounts, no data leaves the browser.
