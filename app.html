The lawn

Sod is cut to order and dies on the pallet - about 24 hours in heat, 48 in cool weather. The clock starts at the farm, not at your curb.

The order

The pallet clock

Sod is harvested to order and dies stacked - 24 hours in heat, 48 cool, starting at the farm. The ground is raked before the truck is called, the crew is ready before it arrives, and the first pallet is laid before it leaves. Seams stagger like brick, edges get rolled flat once, and establishment water is no joke: half an inch a day for two weeks, thousands of gallons for a real lawn.
