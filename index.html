The pallet clock

Cut to order and dying stacked: 24 hours in heat, 48 cool. Ground raked before the truck is called, crew ready before it arrives.

The shape tax

Straight edges waste 5%; curves and angles waste 10% in cut-offs. And pallets come in 450 sq ft - rounding is spare sod, not waste.

The water truth

Establishment is half an inch a day for two weeks - thousands of gallons for a real lawn. Stagger seams like brick, roll it once, keep it wet.
